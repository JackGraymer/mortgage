# Quick-Reference Checklist

Work through these items across the different phases of your purchase.

## Before you start

-   [ ] Check your budget: total affordable price (20% equity, 33% affordability rule)
-   [ ] Gather financial documents (tax returns, salary statements, asset statements)
-   [ ] Verify eligibility (residence permit, Lex Koller if applicable)

## Financing

-   [ ] Get mortgage pre-approval from at least 2–3 banks or a broker
-   [ ] Confirm hard cash equity (10%) is available
-   [ ] Decide on Pillar 2 / Pillar 3a strategy (withdraw vs. pledge)
-   [ ] Understand the stress test (5% interest) and whether you pass
-   [ ] Compare fixed-rate vs. SARON options

## During the search

-   [ ] Visit properties in person
-   [ ] Request property documents: *Grundbuchauszug* (land register extract), floor plans, building-permit history
-   [ ] Check zoning (*Zonenplan*) and any planned developments nearby
-   [ ] Commission a building inspection if the property is older
-   [ ] Check for outstanding liens or easements (*Dienstbarkeiten*)

## Closing

-   [ ] Sign reservation agreement with deposit (ensure financing is confirmed first)
-   [ ] Review the notarial deed with a lawyer or trusted advisor (optional but recommended)
-   [ ] Arrange property insurance (building insurance is mandatory in all cantons)
-   [ ] Prepare funds for closing costs (notary, land register, transfer tax)
-   [ ] Schedule utility transfers (electricity, gas, water, internet, waste)
-   [ ] Notify your municipality of the change of address

## After purchase

-   [ ] Register at the municipality (*Einwohnerkontrolle*)
-   [ ] File tax returns including the new property (imputed rental value, mortgage interest)
-   [ ] Set up amortization payments for the 2nd mortgage
-   [ ] Review mortgage renewal terms 3–6 months before expiry
