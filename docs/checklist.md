# Quick-Reference Checklist

Work through these items across the different phases of your purchase.

## Before you start

-   [ ] Check your budget: total affordable price (20% equity, 33% affordability rule)
-   [ ] Gather financial documents (tax returns, salary statements, asset statements, debt register extract)
-   [ ] Verify eligibility (residence permit, Lex Koller if applicable)
-   [ ] Check your Pillar 2/3a balances and withdrawal/pledge conditions
-   [ ] Decide on target region/canton (taxes, commute, schools, lifestyle)

## Financing

-   [ ] Get mortgage pre-approval from at least 2–3 banks or a broker
-   [ ] Confirm hard cash equity (10%) is available and liquid
-   [ ] Decide on Pillar 2 / Pillar 3a strategy (withdraw vs. pledge)
-   [ ] Understand the stress test (5% interest on full mortgage) and whether you pass
-   [ ] Compare fixed-rate vs. SARON options and consider a split mortgage
-   [ ] Check if your employer offers preferential mortgage rates
-   [ ] Get a bank valuation indication for your target price range

## During the search

-   [ ] Visit properties in person (multiple times, different times of day)
-   [ ] Request property documents: *Grundbuchauszug* (land register extract), floor plans, building-permit history, GEAK/CECB energy certificate
-   [ ] Check zoning (*Zonenplan*) and any planned developments nearby
-   [ ] Commission a building inspection if the property is older (check structure, roof, heating, plumbing, electrical, asbestos, radon)
-   [ ] Check for outstanding liens or easements (*Dienstbarkeiten*)
-   [ ] Verify the property is not in a hazard zone (avalanche, flood, rockfall — check cantonal hazard maps)
-   [ ] For condos (Stockwerkeigentum): review the *Verwaltungsreglement*, renovation fund balance, upcoming special assessments
-   [ ] Check noise exposure (traffic, rail, aircraft — Lärmempfindlichkeitsstufen)
-   [ ] Estimate renovation/energy upgrade costs (heating replacement, insulation, windows, solar)

## Closing

-   [ ] Sign reservation agreement with deposit (ensure financing is confirmed first)
-   [ ] Review the notarial deed with a lawyer or trusted advisor (optional but recommended)
-   [ ] Arrange property insurance (building insurance is mandatory in all cantons — cantonal monopoly or private)
-   [ ] Prepare funds for closing costs (notary, land register, transfer tax, mortgage setup, valuation)
-   [ ] Schedule utility transfers (electricity, gas, water, internet, waste)
-   [ ] Notify your municipality of the change of address (*Einwohnerkontrolle*)
-   [ ] Order mail forwarding (Post)
-   [ ] For condos: confirm handover of keys, garage/parking, cellar, common areas

## After purchase

-   [ ] Register at the municipality (*Einwohnerkontrolle*) within 14 days
-   [ ] File tax returns including the new property (imputed rental value, mortgage interest, maintenance)
-   [ ] Set up amortization payments for the 2nd mortgage (direct or indirect via Pillar 3a)
-   [ ] Review mortgage renewal terms 3–6 months before expiry
-   [ ] Keep all receipts for value-enhancing investments (Anlagekosten) for future gains tax
-   [ ] Plan energy upgrades if GEAK/CECB is D or worse (cantonal deadlines may apply)
-   [ ] Update your will/estate plan to reflect the new asset
-   [ ] Consider household insurance (Hausratversicherung) and liability insurance (Privathaftpflicht)
