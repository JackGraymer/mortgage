# Costs & Fees

The purchase price is only part of the total. Plan for **1–4% of the purchase price** in closing costs on top, depending on your canton.

## Total purchase costs

| Cost item | Typical amount | Payable by | Notes |
| --------- | -------------- | ---------- | ----- |
| Purchase price | 100% of agreed price | Buyer (mortgage + equity) | |
| Notary fees | 0.1–0.5% | Buyer | Regulated tariff; varies by canton |
| Land register entry | 0.1–0.3% | Buyer | |
| Property transfer tax | 0.1–3% (varies by canton) | Buyer | Zurich: ~0.1% (municipal only); Aargau: ~1–1.5%; Geneva: ~3% |
| Real-estate agent commission | 1–3% | Typically the **seller** in CH (always verify) | Only if buyer engages own agent |
| Mortgage setup fee | CHF 500–2,000 | Buyer | One-time; some banks waive for existing clients |
| Property valuation (by bank) | CHF 500–1,500 | Buyer | Required for mortgage; bank chooses appraiser |
| Building insurance transfer | CHF 100–300 | Buyer | Mandatory in all cantons (cantonal monopoly or private) |

**Total closing costs for the buyer (without commission): typically 1–4% of the purchase price**, heavily dependent on the canton.

## Canton-by-canton examples (buyer closing costs, excl. agent commission)

| Canton | Transfer tax | Notary + land register | Mortgage setup + valuation | Buyer closing costs (rough) |
| ------ | ------------ | ---------------------- | -------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| **Zurich** | ~0.1% (municipal) | ~0.5–0.8% | ~CHF 2,000 | **~0.7–1.1%** |
| **Aargau** | ~1–1.5% | ~0.6–1.0% | ~CHF 2,000 | **~1.8–2.8%** |
| **Zug** | ~0.5–1% | ~0.5–0.8% | ~CHF 2,000 | **~1.2–2%** |
| **Lucerne** | ~0.5–1% | ~0.5–0.8% | ~CHF 2,000 | **~1.2–2%** |
| **Schwyz** | Often 0% (municipal) | ~0.5–0.8% | ~CHF 2,000 | **~0.7–1%** |
| **Geneva** | ~3% | ~0.5–1% | ~CHF 2,000 | **~3.5–4.5%** |
| **Vaud** | ~2–3% | ~0.5–1% | ~CHF 2,000 | **~2.5–4%** |

!!! example "Worked example — CHF 1,000,000 in Aargau"
    Transfer tax at ~1.2%: CHF 12,000 · Notary + land register at ~0.8%: CHF 8,000 · Mortgage setup: ~CHF 1,500 · Valuation: ~CHF 1,000 → **≈ CHF 22,500** (~2.25%) of closing costs on top of the price.

## Notary and land register fees

| Fee type | Typical cost |
| -------- | ------------ |
| **Notary fees** | 0.1–0.5% of purchase price (varies by canton; often fixed tariff) |
| **Land register entry (Grundbuch)** | 0.1–0.3% of purchase price |

| Canton | Notary + Land Register (combined, typical) |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------ |
| **Zurich** | ~0.5–0.8% of purchase price (notary ~0.1–0.25%, land register ~0.1–0.3%) |
| **Aargau** | ~0.6–1.0% of purchase price |
| **Zug** | ~0.5–0.8% |
| **Lucerne** | ~0.5–0.8% |

!!! tip "Notary choice"
    In most cantons you can choose your notary. Fees are regulated but can vary slightly. Ask for a cost estimate (Kostenvoranschlag) upfront.

## Ongoing annual costs (budgeting)

| Cost item | Typical annual amount |
| --------- | --------------------- |
| Mortgage interest (actual rate) | 1st mortgage × rate + 2nd mortgage × rate |
| Amortization (2nd mortgage) | 2nd mortgage / amortization term (e.g., 15 years) |
| Maintenance & repairs | ~1% of property value (rule of thumb) |
| Heating / ancillary costs | CHF 3,000–8,000 (house, depends on size, insulation, energy source) |
| Building insurance | CHF 300–800 (cantonal monopoly in many cantons) |
| Property taxes (Liegenschaftssteuer) | Some municipalities levy an annual property tax (e.g., ZH: none; AG: some municipalities) |
| Wealth tax | On net wealth (property tax value − mortgage) — see [Taxation](taxation/index.md) |
| Income tax (imputed rental value) | Net of mortgage interest & maintenance deductions — see [Taxation](taxation/index.md) |

## Budgeting checklist

-   **Equity** — 20% (10% hard cash + 10% Pillar 2/3a) for the deposit and purchase
-   **Closing costs** — 1–4% of the price in cash (varies heavily by canton)
-   **Move-in budget** — moving costs and immediate repairs/renovations
-   **Ongoing costs** — interest, amortization, maintenance, heating, insurance (see [Financing](financing/index.md))
-   **Tax impact** — imputed rental value, wealth tax (see [Taxation](taxation/index.md))
