# Legal & Eligibility

Before looking at properties, it is worth confirming that you are legally allowed to buy — and, if you are a foreign national, under what conditions.

## Who can buy a property

| Status | Can buy? | Conditions |
| ------ | -------- | ---------- |
| Swiss citizen | Yes | No restrictions |
| Foreign national (resident / **C permit**) | Yes | No restrictions |
| Cross-border worker (**G permit**) | Yes | Property must be principal residence |
| Foreign national (**B permit**) | Yes | Property must be principal residence |
| Non-resident foreigner | Conditional | Subject to *Lex Koller* restrictions |

!!! warning
    If you hold a **B permit** or work as a cross-border commuter (**G permit**), you can generally only buy property as your **principal residence** — not as an investment or second home. You must live in the property yourself.

## Lex Koller (BewG)

The *Bundesgesetz über den Erwerb von Grundstücken durch Personen im Ausland* (BewG) restricts property purchases by persons resident abroad. It generally prohibits the acquisition of apartment buildings, single-family homes and condominiums by persons without Swiss citizenship or a permanent residence permit (C permit).

**Key exceptions:**

-   Non-residents may buy commercial properties
-   Non-residents may buy holiday homes in designated tourist areas (*Tourismuszonen*), subject to cantonal quotas (annual quota per municipality)
-   Holders of a C permit or Swiss citizenship face no restrictions
-   **Inheritance:** Non-residents may inherit property but must sell within a reasonable period (usually 1–2 years) unless they qualify for an exception

!!! tip
    If your status changes — for example from B to C permit — your ability to buy (and later your tax position) changes too. Check your permit type before signing anything.

## Second homes (Zweitwohnungen) — Lex Weber / Second Home Initiative

Since 2016, the **Second Home Initiative** (Lex Weber) limits the share of second homes in each municipality to **20%** of total housing stock. In municipalities already above 20%, new second-home construction is largely prohibited. This affects:

-   Purchase of existing second homes: generally allowed (but check municipal quota)
-   New builds: only allowed if municipality is below 20% quota
-   Conversion of principal residence to second home: may require authorization

!!! note "Principal residence vs. second home"
    A "principal residence" (Hauptwohnsitz) is where you are registered with the municipality (*Einwohnerkontrolle*) and spend the majority of your time. Owning a second home has tax implications (no mortgage interest deduction for imputed rental value in some cantons, higher wealth tax).

## Cantonal and municipal regulations

Each canton may impose additional requirements:

-   **Zurich (ZH):** standard rules apply; no additional restrictions for residents
-   **Aargau (AG):** standard rules apply
-   Some cantons levy a *Handänderungssteuer* (property transfer tax) — see [Costs & Fees](../costs.md)

Every municipality has a **Zonenplan** (zoning plan) that defines what can be built where. Always verify zoning before purchasing land or an existing property — it determines whether you may extend, renovate or change the use of the building.

!!! warning
    A *Grundbuchauszug* (land register extract) reveals liens, easements (*Dienstbarkeiten*) and other encumbrances. Always request one before you commit — your notary can arrange it.

## Building regulations & energy certificate (GEAK/CECB)

-   **GEAK / CECB** (Gebäudeenergieausweis der Kantone / Certificat énergétique cantonal du bâtiment): mandatory energy certificate when selling, renting, or doing major renovations in most cantons — check the grade (A–G, A = best) before buying an older property.
-   **Cantonal energy laws:** Most cantons now require renewable heating replacement (heat pump, district heating, wood) when oil/gas boilers fail; deadlines vary by canton.
-   For the buyer-side costs, subsidies and tax deductions on energy upgrades, see [Overview — Energy & renovation requirements](../overview.md) and [Taxation — Deductions](../taxation/index.md).
