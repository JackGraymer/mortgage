# After the Visit

The visit is over — now decide like a buyer, not a fan.

-   **Score it cold** in the [Viewing Scorecard](scorecard.md) right away — impressions fade and candidates blur together after the third house.
-   Use the **24-hour rule**: sleep on it. Plan a **second visit in the evening or on a weekend** to test light and noise, and ideally in rain to see how the house sheds water.
-   Request whatever paperwork is still missing and get the inclusion list confirmed in writing.
-   For anything **pre-1980** or any doubt, commission an independent inspection (engineer, SVIT expert or GEAK advisor — ~CHF 500–1,500). It is routinely the best money of the entire purchase.
-   Re-run your numbers with real inputs: [Amortization Calculator](../financing/calculator.md), [Affordability Calculator](../financing/affordability.md), [Tax Estimator](../taxation/calculator.md).
-   Use documented defects as negotiation leverage (*Preisabzug*) — but in hot markets be ready to act fast; don't lose the house over 1%.
-   After signing a reservation you typically get a **10–14 day protection window**: order the bank valuation, the final Grundbuchauszug, the fire-insurance certificate and verify tenancy *before* the notarial deed. See [Purchase Process](../process.md).

## Rapid decision table

| Finding | Action |
| ------- | ------ |
| Moisture stains or damp smell | Ask for damage history + insurance claims; order an inspection |
| Oil heating, any age | Ask for bills; assume replacement CHF 25,000–40,000 within 5–10 years |
| Single glazing (pre-2000) | Budget windows CHF 15,000–25,000+ |
| Eternit / asbestos materials | Budget removal; never DIY |
| Easement restricting you | Check it is lived-with-able on paper before negotiating |
| Roof or flat-roof membrane > 15–20 yr | Budget CHF 20,000–40,000 |
| Unauthorised extension | Verify it can be regularised (Baubewilligung) or price the risk |
| Price 10%+ under the street | Find out why — in writing — before getting excited |
